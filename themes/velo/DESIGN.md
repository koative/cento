# Vélo design

Mode: Experience with a functional selector at its center.

A steel cycling editorial with a full-bleed bicycle photograph, scarlet race strip, perspective frame geometry configurator, and quiet manifesto.

## Tokens

Bone #f2f0e7 / racing red #d42d22 / graphite #242924.

Typography: Barlow Condensed and DM Sans. Display tracking never below -0.04em. Desktop display is capped at 96px.

## Composition and behavior

Choose Road or All-road, a finish, and a frame size. The geometry, price, saved-build summary, and expandable specifications update together. Saved configurations last only for this visit.

The mobile composition reflows into one column at 760px (750px for Vélo). Decorative perspective responds to hover only where useful. Continuous motion is initiated by the visitor; reduced-motion preferences disable transitions and animation.
