# Prism design

Mode: Experience with a functional selector at its center.

A creative conference expressed through lavender Swiss-influenced type, a perspective folded-paper sculpture, a generous agenda, and a perforated ticket.

## Tokens

Lavender #c8aff1 / deep plum #2b163e / orange #ff8a51.

Typography: Space Grotesk and Inter. Display tracking never below -0.04em. Desktop display is capped at 96px.

## Composition and behavior

Switch days and filter the agenda by track. Choose a pass and quantity (1–4) to see a calculated illustrative total and demo selection confirmation.

The mobile composition reflows into one column at 760px (750px for Vélo). Decorative perspective responds to hover only where useful. Continuous motion is initiated by the visitor; reduced-motion preferences disable transitions and animation.

## Provenance

All folded-paper geometry and speaker artwork are original CSS. No third-party image assets.
